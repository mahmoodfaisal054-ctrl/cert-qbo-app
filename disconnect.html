QuickBooks disconnected

The connection between CERT Bookkeeping Connector and QuickBooks Online has been removed.

  • The app can no longer read or write QuickBooks data.
  • The app deletes its stored access tokens on its next run.
  • Entries already posted remain in QuickBooks Online.

To connect again, use Connect to QuickBooks.
