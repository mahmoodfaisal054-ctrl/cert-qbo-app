CERT. GLOBAL MANAGEMENT F.Z.E

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

This policy explains how CERT. GLOBAL MANAGEMENT F.Z.E ("CERT", "we") handles data in CERT Bookkeeping Connector (the "App"), a private tool used only by CERT for its own bookkeeping.

1. Data we process

  • CERT's own QuickBooks Online accounting data: chart of accounts, vendors, tax codes and transactions.
  • Supplier bills, receipts and bank statements sent to CERT, and the figures read from them.
  • Names and email addresses of CERT staff who submit or approve entries.

We do not store payment card numbers, loyalty numbers, bank account numbers or identity numbers. Where a card is shown, only its last four digits are kept.

2. Why we process it

Only to prepare, approve, post and reconcile CERT's accounting records, and to keep the audit trail required by UAE law. We do not sell, rent or share data for marketing.

3. Service providers

The App relies on: Intuit (QuickBooks Online), Anthropic (Claude, to read documents), Google (email and spreadsheets) and Make.com (automation). Each processes data only to provide its service to CERT, under its own terms and privacy policy.

4. Security

  • QuickBooks access tokens are stored encrypted. Secret keys are never published or shared.
  • Access is limited to authorised CERT staff. Every approval is recorded in an audit log.
  • Nothing is posted to QuickBooks without approval by a named CERT approver.

5. Retention

Accounting records and source documents are kept for at least the period required by UAE tax law (currently seven years), then deleted.

6. Disconnecting

CERT can disconnect the App from QuickBooks at any time. The App then deletes its stored access tokens.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: cert@certificationglobal.com.
