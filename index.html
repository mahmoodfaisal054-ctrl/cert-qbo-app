CERT Bookkeeping Connector

CERT Bookkeeping Connector is a private, internal tool used only by CERT. GLOBAL MANAGEMENT F.Z.E to prepare bookkeeping entries in its own QuickBooks Online company.

What it does

  • Reads supplier bills and receipts sent to the company.
  • Prepares draft entries for review. Nothing is posted until a named finance approver approves it.
  • Attaches the source document to each approved entry in QuickBooks Online.

This tool is not offered to the public or to other companies.
